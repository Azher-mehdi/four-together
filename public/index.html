How to play

Share your room with a friend. Red goes first. Take turns choosing a column to drop a piece into its lowest empty space. Connect four of your pieces horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win. A full board without a winner is a draw.

Both players must approve a rematch. If disconnected, reopen this room in the same browser to rejoin. Inactive disconnected rooms expire after an hour; server restarts clear rooms.
